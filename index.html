A HUMAN BEHIND THE UNIVERSE

Made by one curious
mind. For curious minds.

Hey, I'm Harsheel Mishra, the creator of NullShift.

I'm interested in game development, music, technology, and the big questions that make you pause halfway through a normal day. NullShift is where those interests meet: a growing project built through experiments, sketches, systems, sound, and a lot of thinking.

It's being made one piece at a time. The goal isn't to rush toward a big reveal. It's to make every piece worth discovering.
